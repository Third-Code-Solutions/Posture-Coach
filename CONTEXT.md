# Third Code Posture

Browser-local educational coaching that helps one person position a camera, establish a visible baseline, and practice one evidence-supported cue at a time.

## Language

**Guided setup**:
A short pre-calibration interval that gives the person time to leave the controls and settle inside the full-body frame. No baseline samples belong to guided setup.
_Avoid_: Warm-up, assessment

**Calibration**:
A bounded set of accepted pose observations that establishes a view-specific, person-relative baseline. Calibration is not a posture grade or diagnosis.
_Avoid_: Scan, test, diagnosis

**Coaching**:
Evidence-gated feedback produced only after stable calibration. Coaching may abstain when the visible signal is insufficient.
_Avoid_: Correction, treatment

**Session**:
One continuous camera or local-file practice interval ending when the source stops, finishes, changes, or the page becomes hidden.
_Avoid_: Recording, monitoring
